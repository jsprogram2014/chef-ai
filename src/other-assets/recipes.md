  <h1>Spiced Tomato Curry</h1>
  <p>
    A quick, comforting curry made with fresh tomatoes, turmeric and
    coriander. Ready in about 30 minutes.
  </p>

  <p>
    <strong>Serves:</strong> 4<br />
    <strong>Prep time:</strong> 10 minutes<br />
    <strong>Cook time:</strong> 20 minutes
  </p>

  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>4 large tomatoes, chopped</li>
    <li>1 medium onion, finely chopped</li>
    <li>3 garlic cloves, minced</li>
    <li>1 tbsp ginger, grated</li>
    <li>1 green chili, slit (optional)</li>
    <li>1 tsp turmeric powder</li>
    <li>1 tsp cumin seeds</li>
    <li>1 tsp chili powder</li>
    <li>1 tsp garam masala</li>
    <li>2 tbsp vegetable oil</li>
    <li>½ cup water</li>
    <li>Salt to taste</li>
    <li>A handful of fresh coriander, chopped</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Instructions</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <strong>Heat the oil</strong> in a pan over medium heat. Add the cumin
      seeds and let them sizzle for about 30 seconds.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Add the onion</strong> and cook for 5 minutes until soft and
      golden.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Stir in the garlic, ginger and green chili.</strong> Cook for 1
      minute until fragrant.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Add the spices</strong> — turmeric, chili powder and a pinch of
      salt. Stir for 30 seconds so they don't burn.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Add the tomatoes</strong> and cook for 8–10 minutes, pressing
      them down with a spoon until they break into a thick sauce.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Pour in the water</strong>, bring to a simmer and cook for 5
      more minutes.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Finish</strong> with garam masala and most of the chopped
      coriander. Taste and adjust the salt.
    </li>
  </ol>

  <h2>Serving Suggestions</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Serve hot with rice, chapati or naan.</li>
    <li>Top with the remaining coriander and a spoonful of yogurt.</li>
  </ul>

  <blockquote>
    <strong>Tip:</strong> For a richer curry, add a splash of coconut milk in
    step 6.
  </blockquote>
</section>
